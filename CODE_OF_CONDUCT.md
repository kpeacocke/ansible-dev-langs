# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, constructive, and inclusive project. Treat
people with patience and professionalism, welcome different perspectives, and
focus feedback on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
deliberate disruption, publishing private information, and any conduct that
would make a reasonable contributor feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and
pull requests that violate this standard. Repeated or serious violations may
result in temporary or permanent loss of participation privileges.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer. Do not use a
public issue for sensitive reports.
